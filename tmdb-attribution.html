02

What TMDB Provides

Zenith Movie uses TMDB for:

  • movie and TV metadata, such as titles, descriptions and release information;
  • posters and other images;
  • ratings;
  • account authentication, if you choose to sign in; and
  • watchlist synchronisation for signed-in users.

See the Privacy Policy for how information moves between the App and TMDB.

TMDB Logo

TMDB’s logo identifies the use of TMDB data. Its presence does not mean TMDB endorses Zenith Movie.

04

Independence from TMDB

Zenith Movie is an independent app. It is not endorsed, certified or otherwise approved by TMDB. TMDB’s name and logo belong to TMDB.

05

Content Ownership and Accuracy

Zenith Movie does not own the movie and TV data or images shown in the App. TMDB states that it does not claim ownership of the images or data in its API, and posters, images and other materials belong to their respective rights holders.

TMDB is a community-built database, so details can be incomplete or wrong. Errors are best reported to TMDB, which maintains the data.
